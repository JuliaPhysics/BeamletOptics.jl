Beyond the milestones ​

A 1.0.0 release also needs the following features, which are not assigned to a milestone yet. This definition is arbitrary; the timeframe is on the order of 1-2 years.

  • Beamlet tracing: modeling of the coherence length and its influence on the contrast

  • Polarizing optics based on the Jones formalism: wave plates (λ/2 and λ/4), retarders, Faraday rotators

  • Isolators

  • Modulators: AOM, EOM

  • Visualization: performance updates

  • Documentation: more beginner tutorials, e.g. a beam expander

Get involved ​

Every planned item above is up for grabs. See Contributing for the contribution guidelines and the issue tracker for what is currently being discussed.
